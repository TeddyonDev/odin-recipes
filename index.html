Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and savory seasonings.

This recipe is perfect for family dinners and special occasions. It is hearty, flavorful, and can be prepared ahead of time for convenience.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500g ground beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. Brown the ground beef in a pan.
  4. Add tomato sauce and simmer.
  5. Layer noodles, sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes.
  7. Let cool before serving.

fried rice

Fried rice is a quick meal made with rice, vegetables, and seasonings.

It is simple, tasty, and perfect for busy days.

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Egg
  • Soy sauce
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Cook the vegetables.
  3. Add rice and soy sauce.
  4. Mix in the egg.
  5. Serve hot.

loded fries

Loaded fries are crispy fries topped with cheese and tasty extras.

They make a great snack or side dish.

Ingredients

  • French fries
  • Shredded cheese
  • Bacon bits
  • Sour cream
  • Green onions

Steps

  1. Cook the fries.
  2. Add cheese on top.
  3. Sprinkle bacon and onions.
  4. Add sour cream.
  5. Serve immediately.
